# Nation Confederation Rating Form
No nation matches that search.
Ratings are calculated client-side from data/matches.js, chronologically, using a World-Football-Elo-style formula (see js/elo.js). Add a nation to data/teams.js and its results to data/matches.js to fold it into the ledger — no rebuild step needed. Full instructions in the README.
